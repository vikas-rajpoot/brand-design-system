# MobileAgenticIDE Brand Foundation

## Selected Direction

**Direction C: Work From Anywhere** from [foundation options v4](../options/00-brand-foundation-v4.html).

The promise is that serious work follows the person, not the desk, device, or application. Coding is the flagship technical proof point, but the product also supports research, writing, planning, analysis, and operations.

## Overview

MobileAgenticIDE is a mobile agent workspace and control plane. It connects to a laptop, VPS, or managed Vercel Sandbox and gives people one native surface for prompting, creating, editing, reviewing, approving, and completing agentic work.

The product can run its own agent harness or connect to compatible runtimes such as Claude CLI, Codex, GitHub Copilot, and future integrations. Direct laptop and VPS connections remain distinct from the optional managed sandbox path.

## Positioning

### Category

- **Primary:** Mobile Agent Workspace
- **Architecture:** Agent Control Plane
- **Flagship mode:** Mobile IDE

### Differentiator

One calm mobile workspace spans agents, work types, and execution environments. The user keeps control of consequential actions through structured context, artifacts, permissions, and review states.

### Brand Promise

**Do serious work from anywhere, with any agent, in one mobile workspace.**

### Mission

Make agentic work portable by giving people one clear mobile interface for every agent, workspace, and execution environment.

### Vision

Become the default mobile control surface for human-directed agentic work, from software development to research, writing, planning, and operations.

## Audience

### Primary: The Capable Agent Director

People who want to start meaningful work, provide context, and direct or approve progress from a phone. They value physical freedom, clear state, and control over automation.

### Secondary

- Engineers and tech leads reviewing code, diffs, tests, and deployments.
- Researchers and writers gathering sources, drafting, revising, and organizing work.
- Founders and operators monitoring recurring tasks, analyzing information, and approving actions.

### Audience Boundary

The product is not positioned as a zero-configuration consumer assistant. Workspace setup, permissions, connectivity, and capability boundaries remain explicit until those workflows are simplified.

## Personality

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Professional | Takes work, permissions, and user data seriously. | Corporate or bureaucratic. |
| Minimal | Keeps the work surface focused and uncluttered. | Feature-poor or empty. |
| Technical but legible | Exposes useful detail in understandable structures. | Hiding behind jargon. |
| Premium | Feels deliberate, native, and dependable. | Decorative or status-driven. |
| Calm | Reduces uncertainty through clear state and measured interaction. | Slow, passive, or vague. |
| Open-ended | Supports multiple agents and types of work. | Making universal compatibility claims. |

## Values

1. **Host is truth:** The connected workspace owns execution state.
2. **Private by default:** Direct laptop and VPS connections do not require a MobileAgenticIDE proxy.
3. **Structured over streamed:** Turn agent activity into readable artifacts, events, and decisions.
4. **Resilient by design:** Reconnect without losing context or duplicating actions.
5. **Capabilities are earned:** Destructive actions require explicit enablement and approval.
6. **Runtime choice is yours:** Keep the workspace consistent while allowing the user to choose the agent.
7. **Work travels with you:** The interface should preserve context across places, devices, and work types.

## Voice and Tone

Direct, clear, precise, and unembellished. Explain the system like a senior engineer, but make every state understandable to someone focused on the work rather than the infrastructure.

Use concrete language: **workspace**, **agent**, **runtime**, **artifact**, **review**, **allow**, **deny**, **laptop**, **VPS**, and **sandbox**.

Avoid hype, anthropomorphism, and false certainty. Do not say **fully autonomous**, **set and forget**, **our servers** for direct connections, or **works for everything**.

## Name Usage

### Correct

- MobileAgenticIDE
- MobileAgenticIDE workspace
- MobileAgenticIDE agent harness
- MobileAgenticIDE for iOS

### Incorrect

- Mobile Agentic IDE as three separate product words in product-facing copy
- A generic "AI assistant"
- A claim that all agent runtimes have identical capabilities
- Referring to direct laptop or VPS connections as cloud-hosted by MobileAgenticIDE

Third-party names such as Claude CLI, Codex, and GitHub Copilot remain their respective owners' product names and must be used according to applicable platform guidance.

## Usage

### Do

- Show concrete work: build, research, write, plan, analyze, review, or operate.
- Identify the active runtime and workspace.
- Make permissions, capability differences, and hosted execution explicit.
- Lead with the user's control and the work's portability.

### Do not

- Present the product as coding-only.
- Present it as an unbounded chatbot.
- Hide whether work runs on a laptop, VPS, or managed sandbox.
- Promise unattended execution or universal runtime support.

The IDE that travels with the work.

MobileAgenticIDE is a complete mobile development environment and control plane. Connect to your laptop, a VPS, or a managed Vercel Sandbox, then inspect, prompt, edit, run, review, and ship from your phone.

Core product idea: Your phone is the interface. The laptop, VPS, or sandbox is the execution environment. MobileAgenticIDE lets you move between them without losing context or control.
Direction A

Mobile IDE

The first truly mobile-native IDE for agentic software work.

Strategic focus

Make the phone a serious creation surface: chat with agents, browse files, edit code, run commands, inspect diffs, and approve work.

Cohesion impact

Premium native utility, editor-grade typography, compact navigation, and a strong distinction between local UI and remote execution.

Pros

  • Strongest and clearest category claim.
  • Expands the product beyond monitoring into real work.

Cons

  • Sets a high expectation for editing, terminal, and project workflows.
  • Requires careful scope language while the product matures.
Direction B

One Control Plane

One mobile command surface for every place your code runs.

Strategic focus

Unify laptop, VPS, and managed Vercel Sandbox environments behind one persistent mobile workspace.

Cohesion impact

Structured, trustworthy, infrastructure-aware branding. Connection state, workspace identity, permissions, and resilience become core visual language.

Pros

  • Best long-term platform story and clearest expansion path.
  • Makes the three execution environments feel like one product.

Cons

  • Can sound like infrastructure rather than an IDE.
  • Needs excellent workspace switching to avoid cognitive overload.
Direction C

Build From Anywhere

Your agent keeps building. You keep moving. The whole loop fits in your hand.

Strategic focus

Own the entire remote build loop: prompt, observe, edit, test, review, and deploy while away from the desk.

Cohesion impact

More human and outcome-led, with fast motion, clear progress, and a confident but restrained voice.

Pros

  • Most compelling user promise and App Store narrative.
  • Connects the technical system to reclaimed time and momentum.

Cons

  • Could imply reliable deployment across every environment too early.
  • Must preserve the human approval and privacy story.

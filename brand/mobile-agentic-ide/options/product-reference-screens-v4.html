Inventory and inclusion reason

Every screen has a distinct test job

Strengths

  • Preserves every useful state from v1 and v2.
  • Adds a persistent activity rail with closed and open file-explorer states, plus file viewing, search, terminal detail, and capability disclosure.
  • Tests normal, empty, risky, offline, recovery, and completion states.
  • Gives later visual options one stable comparison surface.

Tradeoffs

  • Seventeen screens take longer to review.
  • Later previews may show a focused subset, while keeping content unchanged.
  • Hosted sandbox and external adapters remain intentionally deferred.

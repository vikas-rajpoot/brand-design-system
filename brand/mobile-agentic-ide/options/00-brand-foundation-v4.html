Decision gate 00 · v4 · expanded scope

One mobile workspace for serious agentic work.

Coding is the flagship proof point, not the boundary. MobileAgenticIDE can connect to a laptop, VPS, or managed Vercel Sandbox, run its own harness or compatible runtimes, and help with code, research, writing, planning, analysis, and operations.

Product center: the phone is the work surface; agents, artifacts, approvals, and execution environments are modular. The user stays in charge of consequential actions.
Direction A

Mobile Agent Workspace

The broad category: one native place to direct agents across technical and non-technical work.

Brand cohesion

Calm, capable, open-ended, and concrete. The interface emphasizes workspaces, artifacts, context, approvals, and clear execution state.

Pros

  • Accurately describes coding plus research, writing, planning, analysis, and operations.
  • Leaves room for the own harness, external adapters, laptop, VPS, and sandbox.

Cons

  • Needs strong examples to avoid sounding like a vague general assistant.
  • “MobileAgenticIDE” may need a future naming extension if IDE feels too narrow.
Direction B

Personal Agent Control Plane

The architecture: one mobile command surface for agents, tools, workspaces, and permissions.

Brand cohesion

Structured, trustworthy, and extensible. Runtime adapters, capability negotiation, connection state, and earned permissions lead the visual language.

Pros

  • Strongest technical architecture and long-term platform story.
  • Fits the first-party harness, Claude CLI, Codex, Copilot, and future runtimes.

Cons

  • More technical and less immediately accessible outside engineering.
  • Must communicate outcomes, not only infrastructure.
Direction C

Work From Anywhere

The promise: serious work follows the person, not the desk, device, or application.

Brand cohesion

Human, direct, and spacious, with concrete workflow stories: build, research, write, plan, analyze, and operate from a phone.

Pros

  • Strongest emotional promise and easiest broad-market explanation.
  • Preserves coding while making the product useful beyond code.

Cons

  • Can sound like generic productivity software without the agent/workspace proof.
  • Needs careful language around automation and hosted execution.
Selection needed: Confirm mobile-agentic-ide and choose A, B, or C. A useful combination is “A as category, B as architecture, C as promise.”

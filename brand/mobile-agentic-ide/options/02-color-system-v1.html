Decision gate 02 · v1 · color system

Quiet surfaces. Clear consequences.

Three palette directions for MobileAgenticIDE. Each keeps the work surface restrained while making connection state, permissions, warnings, and destructive actions immediately legible.

Shared constraint: dark mode is the primary technical environment; light mode must remain a complete, high-contrast working mode. Green means allowed or connected, amber means review or caution, and red is reserved for destructive danger.
